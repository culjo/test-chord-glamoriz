# Glamoriz: business profile

All details are made up for the TSG Labs exercise.

## Who we are
Glamoriz is a Nigerian skincare vendor founded in 2021, selling mainly through WhatsApp and Instagram.
We stock targeted skincare for melanin-rich skin: cleansers, serums, moisturisers and sunscreens.
Customers are mostly women aged 20–40 in Lagos and Abuja who buy again every one to two months.

- Lagos pickup point: Lekki Phase 1 (by appointment)
- Abuja pickup point: Wuse 2 (by appointment)
- Human team hours: Monday–Saturday, 9am–6pm (WAT). Outside these hours, tell the customer a teammate
  will reply by 10am the next working day.

## Tone
Warm, knowledgeable, honest. We'd rather sell the right ₦9,000 product than the wrong ₦30,000 routine.
We never shame anyone's skin and never promise results like "clears acne in 7 days".
